Privacy & Data Security

We prioritize your privacy. All visual processing is performed entirely on your device.

  • This application never stores, records, or shares videos, photos, or screenshots of your face or workspace. The camera functions solely as a real-time sensor.
  • All posture analysis and fatigue detection are processed locally on your computer using an intelligent system. Visual data is never transmitted over the internet or shared with third parties.
  • The only data we store consists of statistical logs comprising numbers and plain text which are displayed in the History tab.
  • Cloud services (AWS) are used exclusively to synchronize your preference settings and nonvisual activity summaries, ensuring your configurations are preserved if you switch devices.
  • The camera indicator light (green/white) on your device will remain illuminated during calibration and active sessions, ensuring you always have full control over when the camera is in use.
